# Company Evaluation Criteria

Criteria for evaluating and scoring potential companies to outreach to.

## Must Have (Required)

### 1. Active Hiring

- Has current job openings for developers
- Recently posted positions (past 30 days preferred)
- "We're hiring" or "Join our team" language

### 2. Remote-Friendly Policy

Must have ONE of:

- "remote worldwide"
- "work from anywhere"
- "hiring globally"
- "no location restrictions"
- "distributed team"
- "remote first"

## Should Have (High Weight)

### 3. Tech Stack Match

Preferred technologies (at least ONE):

- Vue.js or Nuxt.js
- React or Next.js
- TypeScript
- Laravel or PHP
- Node.js
- SaaS architecture

### 4. Offshore/LATAM Friendly

Positive indicators:

- Already employs LATAM developers
- Has "LATAM" or "Latin America" in hiring
- Mentions specific countries (Mexico, Colombia, Argentina, Cuba, etc.)
- Timezone compatibility (UTC-5 to UTC-3)

## Nice to Have (Medium Weight)

### 5. Growth Stage

Positive indicators:

- Series A or later funding
- 50+ employees
- VC-backed
- "Fast-growing" or "rapidly scaling"

### 6. Company Type

Positive:

- Product company (not just staffing)
- B2B SaaS preferred
- Tech-forward industry

## Red Flags (Skip If)

- Only hiring on-site
- "Must be in US" or similar restrictions
- No remote policy mentioned
- Only contracting (no full-time)
- Suspicious or unclear business model

## Pass/Fail Questions

For each company, answer:

1. Do they have open developer positions?
2. Is remote work allowed worldwide?
3. Do they hire in LATAM or have LATAM team?
4. Do they use my tech stack?
5. Is there a way to contact them (email)?

**If answer to #1-3 is yes AND #5 is yes → Proceed**
