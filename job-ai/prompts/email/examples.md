# Email Examples

Real examples of outreach emails - use these as reference.

## Good Examples

### Example 1: SaaS Company

```
Subject: SaaS developer interest + Latin America remote question

Hi there,

I came across Deel and was impressed by how you're simplifying global payroll and compliance.

I've built multi-tenant SaaS platforms for 40+ enterprise clients, with a focus on performance and clean architecture. Recently optimized a React app's Core Web Vitals from 3.2s to 1.4s LCP.

I'd love to learn more about opportunities for remote developers in Latin America.

Best,
Rayko Azcue
Senior Full-Stack Developer
```

### Example 2: Startup

```
Subject: Vue.js + startup experience question

Hi,

Your work on [specific feature/product] caught my attention. I'm a senior Vue.js developer who has built similar platforms from scratch.

I noticed you mention being remote-first. I'm based in Cuba (UTC-5) and work with US/EU teams regularly.

Would you have time for a quick chat about potential opportunities?

Best,
Rayko
```

### Example 3: Direct Application

```
Subject: Senior Frontend Developer Application - Rayko Azcue

Dear Hiring Team,

I am excited to apply for the Senior Frontend Engineer position at [Company].

With 7+ years building scalable web applications and deep expertise in Vue.js and React, I believe I could make an immediate impact on your team. Recently, I led a migration that reduced load times by 45% while improving Core Web Vitals.

I have attached my resume and would welcome the opportunity to discuss how my experience would benefit [Company].

Thank you for your consideration.

Best regards,
Rayko Azcue
```

## Bad Examples (Avoid These)

### Bad Example 1: Too Generic

```
Subject: Job Application

Dear Sir/Madam,

I am writing to apply for the developer position. I have experience with many programming languages and frameworks. I am hardworking and dedicated. Please consider me for any available positions.

Thank you.
```

**Why it's bad:** No personalization, too formal, no specific achievements, too passive.

### Bad Example 2: Overly Long

```
Subject: Application for Software Engineer Position

Dear Hiring Manager,

I am writing to express my interest in the Software Engineer position at your esteemed company. I have been working in software development for over 7 years and during this time I have worked with many technologies including Vue.js, React, Angular, Node.js, Python, Java, PHP, Laravel, Django, Express, MongoDB, PostgreSQL, MySQL, Redis, Docker, Kubernetes, AWS, GCP, Azure, CI/CD, Agile, Scrum, and many more...

[continues with list of every skill and job]
```

**Why it's bad:** Way too long, overwhelming, reads like a resume dump.

### Bad Example 3: Desperate Tone

```
Subject: PLEASE consider me for any position you have!

Hi,

I really need a job and I saw your company online. I am a developer and I know many things. I am from Cuba and I really need to work remotely. I will do any task you give me for very low pay if needed. Please help me!

Thank you,
Desperate Developer
```

**Why it's bad:** Desperate tone, apologizing for location, suggesting low pay, not confident.

### Bad Example 4: Apologetic

```
Subject: Sorry to bother you - job inquiry (if you have time)

Hi,

I hope this email finds you well. I apologize for reaching out uninvited. I am a developer from Cuba and I know you are probably very busy...

[continues with weak value prop]
```

**Why it's bad:** Apologetic tone, weak opener, no confidence.

## Key Takeaways

**Good emails:**

- Specific to the company
- Mention one relevant achievement
- Confident, not apologetic
- Short (~100 words)
- Clear call to action

**Bad emails:**

- Generic/openers
- List all skills
- Apologize for location
- Too long or too short
- Passive/desperate tone
