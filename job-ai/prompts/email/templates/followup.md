# Follow-up Email Template

Use this template when following up on a previous outreach email.

## Timing

Wait 5-7 days before following up.
Send only ONE follow-up.
If no response after follow-up, move on.

## Template

```
Subject: Re: {original_subject}

Hi {recipient_name or "there"},

{short_follow_up}

Looking forward to connecting.

Best,
Rayko Azcue
```

## Follow-up Lines

Choose one:

- "Just wanted to bump this up in your inbox - still very interested in {Company}."
- "Following up on my previous email about opportunities at {Company}."
- "I know you're busy, but wanted to see if you had any questions."

## Anti-Patterns

- Don't be pushy
- Don't say "I haven't heard back"
- Don't guilt-trip
- Don't send more than 2 emails total (initial + 1 follow-up)
