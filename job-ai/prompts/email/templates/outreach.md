# Cold Outreach Email Template

Use this template when reaching out to companies without active job postings.

## Template

```
Subject: {catchy_subject_line}

Hi {recipient_name or "there"},

{customized_opening_1_sentence}

{customized_value_prop_1_2_sentences}

I'd love to learn more about your team and how I might contribute.

Best,
Rayko Azcue
Senior Full-Stack Developer
7+ years building scalable web applications
```

## Subject Line Ideas

Generate a subject that is:

- Specific (company name)
- Not generic "Job Application"
- Short (under 60 chars)

Good examples:

- "Vue.js project + Latin America developer interest"
- "Full-stack dev with SaaS experience - {Company}"
- "Quick question about {Company}'s tech stack"

Bad examples:

- "Job Application" (too generic)
- "I am interested in working at your company" (desperate)
- Long subject lines over 60 characters

## Opening Lines

Choose one that connects to their company:

- "I came across {Company} and was impressed by your approach to {specific_product_aspect}."
- "Your work on {specific_feature} caught my attention."
- "I noticed {Company} is building in the {sector} space, and I wanted to reach out."
- "As someone who has built {similar_product_type}, your mission resonated with me."

## Value Proposition Examples

Based on what you know about the company:

- SaaS: "I've built multi-tenant SaaS platforms serving 40+ enterprise clients, focusing on performance and scalability."
- E-commerce: "I've developed e-commerce solutions handling high traffic with optimized checkout flows."
- FinTech: "I understand the importance of compliance and security in financial applications."
- Performance: "I've reduced page load times by 45% and improved Core Web Vitals from 3.2s to 1.4s LCP."

## Customization Per Company

Always include:

1. Specific thing you noticed about their company
2. One achievement relevant to what they do
3. Brief, not a resume
