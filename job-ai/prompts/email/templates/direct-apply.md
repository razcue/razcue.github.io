# Direct Application Email Template

Use this template when applying to a job posting directly.

## When to Use

- Company has active job posting
- Application submitted via email (not portal)
- Need to include resume

## Template

```
Subject: {Position Title} Application - {Your Name}

Dear {hiring_manager_name or "Hiring Team"},

I am excited to apply for the {position_title} position at {company_name}.

{1-2 sentences on why this role/company specifically}

{optional: 1 brief achievement if highly relevant}

I have attached my resume and would welcome the opportunity to discuss how
my experience in {relevant_skills} would benefit your team.

Thank you for your consideration.

Best regards,
Rayko Azcue
```

## Notes

- Keep it shorter than cold outreach (they're busy)
- Focus on the role, not generic interest
- Mention attached resume
- Professional tone
